# Governance

## Today

I'm James Conway ([@jamesconwaydev](https://github.com/jamesconwaydev)). I
started TachyonCMS, and while the project is small I make the final call on
decisions. They happen in public: in issues, Discussions and pull requests.

## Commitments that don't change

- **TachyonCMS stays open source.** It's licensed under the MIT License, and
  that won't change.
- **No CLA, ever, and no copyright assignment.** You keep the copyright to
  your work and sign off under the Developer Certificate of Origin. Because
  copyright stays with everyone who contributed, no single person or company
  can take the project closed.
- **Sponsors get visibility, not control.** Every feature lives in the open
  source project. Nobody can pay for a feature that's held back from
  everyone else.

## Maintainers

Anyone who contributes steadily and reviews other people's work can be
invited to become a maintainer. Once there are at least two maintainers:

- pull requests need an approving review from a maintainer other than the author
- decisions are made by consensus, falling back to a majority vote of maintainers

The list of maintainers will live in this file.

## Where this is headed

Once TachyonCMS has maintainers from more than one organization, the plan is
to move the project, its trademark and its domains to a neutral foundation.
That way it doesn't depend on any one person or company.
